About Me

The person behind the code

Hi, I'm Kris

I'm Chrisper Mwangi Wambui, a Computer Science student at Egerton University (Class of 2027) and a full-stack developer. I enjoy turning ideas into working software, from analytics dashboards to booking systems and online stores.

During my attachment at Skyfalke Technologies in Nairobi, I built WorkPulse, a full-stack attendance and leave management system that replaces paper registers and manual leave forms. It has separate dashboards for employees, managers and admins, smart clock-in with late detection, a leave approval workflow with email notifications, and PDF and CSV reports. I built it with React, Express and MySQL.

I've now Started the Full Stack Development program at Emobilis to strengthen my fundamentals from the ground up, and I'm open to internships and collaborations.
